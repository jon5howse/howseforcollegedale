How this record is built. Each row is extracted from the commission's approved meeting minutes (published in the official agenda packets and on the city website) and shows the motion text and roll call exactly as recorded. Tallies show yeas–nays; abstentions aren't counted in the tally. Routine procedural motions — approving prior minutes and adjournment — are omitted for readability. Votes taken without a roll call in the minutes aren't individually attributable and are not listed. The newest meetings link to the city's video archive until their direct video links are posted.
